Before you book

Frequently asked questions

What is included in the price?

The fare, taxes, and the items listed under your chosen fare tier. Anything extra is shown before you pay — never added silently afterwards.

Can I change my dates after booking?

Yes on Flexible and Premium fares, free up to 48 hours before departure. Essential fares can be changed for a fee, subject to availability.

Do I need to pay now?

This page is a planning and selection flow. Payment happens only on the final booking step, after you review times, travellers, and the total.

What if my flight time changes?

We notify you by email and show the updated timetable in your booking. Flexible and Premium fares can be rebooked free if the change is significant.

Ready to book?

You have landed, searched, and picked a destination. The final step is to confirm travellers, review the total, and book — starting from the destination and fare you chose above.
