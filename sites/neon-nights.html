Before you go

FAQ

Is the festival all ages?

Yes. Children under 12 enter free with a ticket-holding adult. The event is rain or shine.

Can I change my day after buying a single-day ticket?

Single-day tickets can be moved to another day before August 1, subject to availability for that day.

What can I bring?

One sealed water bottle, a small bag, and a rain jacket. Outside food, glass, and professional cameras are not permitted.

Is the site accessible?

Yes. Step-free routes, accessible viewing areas at both stages, and accessible toilets are on site.
