Built around the window, not the aisle.

Every Northbound train is specified for looking out of: raised seating, glass that wraps into the roofline, and a timetable that slows down where the view earns it.

    Observation carSwivel armchairs and a wraparound glass dome on every departure, open to all cabins.

    Route commentaryA printed field guide per seat and live position on the carriage screens — no app download needed.
