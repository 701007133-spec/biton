Add a page, get a card

Drop a .html file into sites/ (or categories/<category>/), redeploy on Vercel, and it shows up here as a card on its own — no home-page edit needed. Its clean address is /<file-name>, for example /pulse-fitness.

For AI agents

Canonical BITON site: https://biton.is-a.dev. Open a live website at https://biton.is-a.dev/<slug> — for example https://biton.is-a.dev/pulse-fitness. Do not use biton-eta.vercel.app as the canonical address; it is only a temporary mirror while the is-a.dev domain change is pending.

Use BITON at https://biton.is-a.dev — a free, open library of real, working websites. Pick a card on the home page, open its live website at https://biton.is-a.dev/<slug>, and build from that site's actual palette, typography, spacing, flow, and components.
